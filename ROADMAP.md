# CyberAtlas roadmap

**Current stage: v0.1 — initial research preview.** This is a direction for continued work, not a promised release schedule. Definitions, structure and proposed explanations remain open to revision.

## Expand where a distinction changes a decision

Investigate the 32 open frontiers and deepen mechanisms where a new child changes a test, decision, assumption, or failure explanation. More nodes are not an end in themselves. Preserve parent context and explain why an addition cannot be represented by an existing node.

## Refine definitions and system boundaries

Review ambiguous terms, inconsistent granularity, overloaded labels, and unstated assumptions. State the object, observer frame, operating conditions, and time scale. When a definition changes, review dependent nodes, relations, and measurements rather than changing the label alone.

## Revisit causal relationships

Audit which links represent containment, dependencies, temporal order, proposed mechanisms, or evidence-supported effects. For causal hypotheses, record the intervening process, enabling conditions, alternative explanations, possible confounders, and observations that would challenge the claim. Qualify or remove relations whose support does not justify their interpretation.

The goal is a more inspectable explanation of causation, not a graph that labels every connection causal. No fixed number of links or validation date is promised.

## Add evidence with a bounded claim

Connect scoped observations to exact configurations, methods, timestamps, artifacts, limitations, and relevant nodes. Keep synthetic examples separate from organizational evidence. An observation that challenges a mechanism is useful input, not an error to hide.

## Improve measurements and language

Refine metrics after the decision, population, denominator, time window, missing-data behavior, and consequences are explicit. Expand English explanations with technical review while retaining stable IDs and useful Vietnamese aliases.

## Make revisions traceable

Continue using validated patches and immutable snapshots. Document splits, merges, corrections and retired claims. Refresh exports and navigation after accepted changes; run checks appropriate to the changed behavior.

A revision is ready when its meaning is explicit, relationships are internally consistent, evidence status is honest, and a reader can identify what would make the claim change again.
